Powder rooms

Venetian plaster in a powder room

Dark, seamless walls with no tile grid, finished by hand with niches that carry their own light. These are real photos of our plaster master’s work, taken while the room was in progress. See the black marmorino powder room.

Plaster in wet areas, in short. Mineral plaster is breathable and does not peel the way paint can. It is much harder for mold to grow on it, though it is not mold-proof, and it is water-resistant when sealed. Powder rooms and bathroom walls outside the direct spray get a protective sealer and need a working exhaust fan. Inside the shower, tile or stone is often the better choice. Plaster projects start at $10,000; see what Venetian plaster costs in Miami and how it holds up in Miami humidity.

Process

How it works, in five steps

  1. Site visit (about a week). We measure, look at what is behind the walls and agree what you want.
  2. Concept (about 1–2 weeks). Two or three directions for layout and style.
  3. Design, 3D and budget (about 2–3 weeks). Layout, 3D, materials and the itemized budget.
  4. Approvals and construction. Building paperwork first, then the work per the estimate, with weekly photo updates and milestone payments.
  5. Handover. Final cleaning, a walkthrough, a punch-list and a 1-year written warranty.

FAQ

Bathroom renovation in Miami: questions

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Miami?

Typically $8–14K for a powder room or refresh, $18–35K for a full bathroom and $38–70K for a luxury primary bathroom in 2026. It depends on the layout, the moisture protection, the stone or tile and the fixtures. We give an itemized price after seeing the space.

Can you replace a tub with a walk-in shower?

Often, yes, where the layout allows it. The drain, the floor level, the glass and the ventilation are planned together, and in a condo the building's rules apply too. One of our apartment projects has a black-framed walk-in shower beside a long stone vanity; we check what is possible in your room at the site visit.

How do you design a small bathroom?

By taking things off the floor and out of the way: a wall-hung WC, a floating vanity, a glass screen instead of a curtain, and niches lit from within so the room needs almost no overhead light. Our 581 sq ft one-bedroom has two new bathrooms in a compact plan. Every layout is drawn for the room, and you see it in 3D before anything is demolished.

Do I need my condo association's approval for a bathroom renovation in Miami?

In most buildings, yes, once the work goes beyond cosmetic changes. A bathroom usually involves plumbing, electrical and ventilation, so check your building's rules and submit the plans before work starts. Every association has its own process. We prepare the paperwork and coordinate with the building; the exact requirements come from your association.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Miami-Dade?

Generally yes when the work moves plumbing, changes electrical circuits or alters walls. A purely cosmetic refresh generally does not. Permits are pulled by our licensed & insured Florida contractor partners, who work under their own permit and contract. Check with your building department and your association if you are unsure.

Can Venetian plaster go in a bathroom or powder room?

Yes. Venetian plaster and marmorino are common in powder rooms and on bathroom walls outside the direct spray, with a protective sealer and a working exhaust fan. Mineral plaster is breathable and does not peel the way paint can, it is much harder for mold to grow on it (it is not mold-proof), and it is water-resistant when sealed. Inside the shower, tile or stone is often the better choice, and we decide on site.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

It depends on the size, the scope and the building's approval process. The design stage takes roughly 4–6 weeks, from the first consultation to the design, 3D and itemized budget. The construction time follows the estimate, with weekly photo updates. You get the timeline upfront, in writing, before you sign.

Do you do bathroom design in Miami, or only the renovation?

Both. Layout, stone or tile, fixtures and lighting are planned as one design, with 3D and an itemized budget. Design can be bought on its own or as part of a full renovation, and we state the fee in writing after the site visit.
